Retail of eyewear, apparel and regional handicrafts in Duque de Caxias, Brazil

We are a retail operation established in Duque de Caxias since 2013, dedicated to the direct sale of eyewear items, apparel, accessories, costume jewelry and handcrafted products from the Baixada Fluminense region of Rio de Janeiro state.

About us

Zerezes Design, Produção e Comércio de Artesanatos S.A. is a privately held Brazilian corporation in regular activity since July 1st, 2013, with its own headquarters in Duque de Caxias, Rio de Janeiro state.

Our operation is fully in-person and conducted internally by our own team. We serve residents of the Baixada Fluminense area and the greater Rio de Janeiro metropolitan region through our physical store, offering products from the four commercial lines registered on our tax records.

We do not work with lead resale, dropshipping, affiliate marketing or any form of commercial intermediation. All sales are conducted directly between our company and the end consumer, at our physical store.

Products and services

Our commercial operation covers four activities registered on our Brazilian Federal Revenue records:

Eyewear items

Primary activity - CNAE 47.74-1-00. We sell ready-made frames, sunglasses, pre-made reading glasses, cases, cords, cleaning cloths and other popular eyewear accessories. We do not perform vision exams nor manufacture prescription lenses.

Apparel and accessories

CNAE 47.81-4-00. We carry adult apparel, belts, bags, wallets, scarves and fashion accessories for direct sale to walk-in customers at our physical store.

Souvenirs, costume jewelry and handicrafts

CNAE 47.89-0-01. Handcrafted pieces made by artisans from the Baixada Fluminense area and the state of Rio de Janeiro, costume jewelry, necklaces, bracelets, earrings and small regional keepsakes.

Office and administrative support services

CNAE 82.11-3-00. Combined administrative support services for small local merchants, including document organization, reception assistance and data entry.
