Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Zerezes Design, Produção e Comércio de Artesanatos S.A., a Brazilian legal entity registered under CNPJ 18.841.936/0001-00, collects, uses and protects personal information from visitors to our website and customers of our physical store in Duque de Caxias, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, in compliance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD - Law No. 13,709/2018).

1. Data we collect

Our website is predominantly institutional and informational. We collect personal data only when the data subject actively provides it through the contact form or by direct communication with our team. The data collected may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Content of the message sent

We also collect technical browsing data in aggregated and non-identifiable form, such as IP address, browser type, operating system and pages visited, exclusively for security and statistical analysis of the site.

2. Purpose of processing

We use the collected data exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Respond to information requests, questions and complaints
  • Provide commercial service and after-sales support
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Improve the browsing experience on the site
  • Prevent fraud and ensure the security of our systems

3. Legal basis

The processing of personal data conducted by us is supported by the legal grounds set forth in Article 7 of the LGPD, notably the consent of the data subject, compliance with a legal or regulatory obligation and legitimate interest.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, assign or trade personal data of our customers or visitors. We do not share data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Any sharing will occur only when necessary to comply with a legal obligation, judicial order or request from a competent authority.

5. Storage and security

The collected data is stored in a controlled environment, with technical and administrative measures appropriate to protect against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or destruction. We retain the data only for the period necessary to fulfill the declared purposes or as required by applicable law.

6. Rights of the data subject

Under the terms of the LGPD, the holder of personal data has the following rights:

  • Confirmation of the existence of processing
  • Access to data
  • Correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data
  • Anonymization, blocking or elimination of unnecessary data
  • Portability of data
  • Elimination of data processed based on consent
  • Revocation of consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contato@zerezesdesign.online or by phone at +55 (21) 3193-1225.

7. Cookies

Our site may use essential cookies for functionality and anonymous analytical cookies. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

8. Changes to this policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will take effect from publication on this page. We recommend periodic review.
